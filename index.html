Nox App

A private, single-user personal assistant integration.

Nox App is personal software. It is built and operated for a single individual — its own developer — to automate access to that person's own email and calendar. It is not a public product, it has no other users, and it is not distributed or offered for sign-up.

What it does

Nox App connects to Google Workspace APIs to help its operator manage their own account: reading and organizing their inbox, and reading and creating entries on their calendar. All processing happens locally. No data is transmitted to any third party.

Google user data

Nox App requests access to Google APIs strictly on behalf of the account owner. Credentials and any retrieved data are stored only on the operator's own machine and are never shared, sold, or used for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning models.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
